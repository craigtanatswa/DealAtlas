## Construction tenders UK: what counts as a public works contract?

A public construction contract is any works contract a public body puts out to suppliers. Most public bodies in England, Wales and Northern Ireland follow the [Procurement Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/54/contents), which came into force on 24 February 2025. The government's [guidance on the Act](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents) explains the rules buyers work to.

Works contracts above £5,193,000 fall under the full rules of the Act. Smaller jobs still get advertised, and they are often a better fit for SMEs.

You'll need to register on the government's central digital platform before you bid, so find the right opportunity on [DealAtlas](/deals) first.
