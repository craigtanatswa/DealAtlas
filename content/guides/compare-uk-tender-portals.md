If you search for government contracts in the UK, you will often see two Cabinet Office services side by side. Since 24 February 2025, new procurements under the Procurement Act 2023 go on Find a Tender, the central digital platform for public procurement. Contracts Finder still carries notices and award records for work that started under the older rules. This guide covers what each service does now, the 2026 thresholds, and where to look.

## The short answer

Use [Find a Tender](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/) for new opportunities from buyers in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. It is free to search, and it is where above-threshold and advertised below-threshold notices appear under the Act. Use [Contracts Finder](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/) when you need older-regime opportunities or award records for procurements that began before 24 February 2025. Add the national portals for Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland when you sell into those administrations.

## What Find a Tender is now

Find a Tender is the government's [central digital platform for procurement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-short-guides/central-digital-platform-factsheet-html). An enhanced version launched on 24 February 2025, when the Procurement Act 2023 took effect for new procurements.

On the platform, contracting authorities publish the notices the Act requires across the life of a contract, from early pipeline information through to award and later changes. Suppliers can search for free, set alerts on the service itself, register once, store core business information and share that information when a buyer asks for it. You still submit the bid in the form the buyer names, often through its own e-tendering system, and you may need to pass a share code or a downloaded file from Find a Tender with your response.

The [Find a Tender search page](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/search) states that, from 24 February 2025, both above-threshold and below-threshold notices about new UK procurements are published there, except below-threshold notices in Scotland. For procurements that started before that date, Find a Tender continues to show the above-threshold notices that sat under the older rules, usually over £139,688 including VAT.

## What Contracts Finder does after the Procurement Act 2023

[Contracts Finder](https://www.gov.uk/contracts-finder) was the main place to search for lower-value English and non-devolved opportunities under the Public Contracts Regulations 2015. You can still search it, create an account and look up past tenders and awards.

[PPN 019](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ppn-019-requirements-to-publish-on-contracts-finder/update-to-legal-requirements-to-publish-procurement-information-on-contracts-finder) updates the publication rules after the Act. It applies only to procurements that commenced under the Public Contracts Regulations 2015. For those procurements, buyers should still publish award information on Contracts Finder when they publish a contract award notice on Find a Tender, when they award under a framework set up under the 2015 regulations, and when they award a below-threshold contract under those rules. New opportunities under the Act, whether above or below threshold, only need notices on Find a Tender. There should be no ongoing requirement to publish new opportunity notices on Contracts Finder from 24 February 2025.

Contracts Finder remains open. It still covers the older regime: opportunities that were already running, awards and call-offs that still follow the 2015 rules, and the historical record for research.

From [1 May 2027](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/746/made), that older-regime publication duty changes again. The Procurement (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/746) defer regulation 5 of the Procurement (Amendment) Regulations 2026 so that, from that date, saved Public Contracts Regulations 2015 rules stop requiring publication on Contracts Finder and, where relevant, replace it with publication on the central digital platform. Until then, Contracts Finder carries those older records.

## Find a Tender vs Contracts Finder at a glance

Here is the difference between Find a Tender and Contracts Finder in practice.

| | Find a Tender | Contracts Finder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role now | Central digital platform for Procurement Act 2023 noticing | Older portal for Public Contracts Regulations 2015 noticing and awards |
| New opportunities from 24 Feb 2025 | Yes, above and below threshold (except below threshold in Scotland) | No new Act opportunity notices |
| Older-regime awards and call-offs | Above-threshold notices that started before 24 Feb 2025 still appear | Still used for PCR awards, framework call-offs and below-threshold awards under the old rules |
| Supplier registration | Register once, store and share core information | Account for saved searches and email updates |
| Cost to search | Free | Free |
| Main users of the record | Anyone bidding for new public contracts in England, Wales and Northern Ireland | Anyone researching older awards or tracking PCR-era frameworks |

Both services are official Cabinet Office tools. Neither replaces the buyer's own e-tendering system, where documents and submissions usually live.

## Thresholds that decide what must be published in 2026

The contract's estimated value including VAT decides whether the full Act applies. [PPN 023](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ppn-023-2026-threshold-amounts/ppn-023-2026-threshold-amounts-html) sets the amounts for procurements that start on or after 1 January 2026 and run to 31 December 2027.

| Contract type | Threshold including VAT |
| --- | --- |
| Goods or services for a central government authority | £135,018 |
| Goods or services for any other public buyer | £207,720 |
| [Works](/categories/construction-infrastructure) | £5,193,000 |
| Light touch contracts | £663,540 |
| Utilities goods or services (not works) | £415,440 |

A contract at or above its threshold is a public contract and needs the full noticing sequence on Find a Tender. Below the threshold, lighter rules apply. If a buyer advertises a notifiable below-threshold contract, it must first publish a below-threshold tender notice on the central digital platform. That duty applies at £12,000 including VAT for most central government authorities, including NHS trusts and foundation trusts, and at £30,000 for other buyers ([Below-threshold contracts guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-define-phase/guidance-below-threshold-contracts-html)). A contract details notice is still required after award at those same values, even when the buyer never advertised the opportunity.

The higher works threshold is why many [construction and infrastructure](/categories/construction-infrastructure) contracts are advertised under the below-threshold rules.

The £139,688 figure on some help text is the older above-threshold reference for procurements that started before the Act. For new work, use the PPN 023 amounts above.

## Devolved portals you still need

Find a Tender and Contracts Finder do not cover every UK buyer on their own.

- Scottish authorities mostly buy under Scotland's own procurement law. Below-threshold Scottish notices are not published on Find a Tender. Use Public Contracts Scotland.
- Welsh public bodies use Sell2Wales alongside Find a Tender.
- Northern Ireland uses eSourcing NI and eTendersNI.

Many buyers also run documents and submissions through a private e-tendering system. A notice on Find a Tender or Contracts Finder is often only the front door. ## How to use both services

Start every search on Find a Tender. That is where new Act notices live. Keep Contracts Finder open when you care about older awards, PCR-era frameworks or historic results. Add the devolved portals for Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, then follow each notice into the buyer's own system for the documents. For [construction and infrastructure](/categories/construction-infrastructure) work, check both early pipeline notices and below-threshold tender notices, because many works contracts sit under the lighter rules.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between Find a Tender and Contracts Finder?

Find a Tender is the central digital platform for public procurement. Since 24 February 2025 it carries new above-threshold and advertised below-threshold notices for buyers in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Contracts Finder is the older portal. It still holds notices and award records for procurements that started under the Public Contracts Regulations 2015, but new Procurement Act opportunities are published on Find a Tender, not Contracts Finder.

### Is Contracts Finder still used after the Procurement Act 2023?

Yes, for procurements that began under the previous rules. Buyers still publish award information on Contracts Finder for Public Contracts Regulations 2015 procurements, including awards under older frameworks and dynamic purchasing systems, and for below-threshold awards made under those rules. New procurements that started on or after 24 February 2025 only need notices on Find a Tender.

### What is the central digital platform for procurement?

The central digital platform is the enhanced Find a Tender service launched on 24 February 2025. Contracting authorities publish the notices required by the Procurement Act 2023 there. Suppliers can search opportunities for free, register once, store core business information and share it when they bid.

### Which 2026 thresholds decide whether a notice goes on Find a Tender?

For procurements that start between 1 January 2026 and 31 December 2027, the main thresholds including VAT are £135,018 for central government goods and services, £207,720 for other public buyers, £5,193,000 for works, £663,540 for light touch contracts and £415,440 for utilities goods or services. Advertised below-threshold contracts at or above £12,000 (most central government authorities) or £30,000 (other buyers) also need a notice on Find a Tender.

### Do I still need Contracts Finder if I already use Find a Tender?

Yes, if you want a full picture. Find a Tender covers new Procurement Act notices. Contracts Finder still holds older-regime opportunities and award records that are useful for research and for call-offs from frameworks set up before 24 February 2025. Scottish, Welsh and Northern Ireland buyers also publish on their own national portals.
