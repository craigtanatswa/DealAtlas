**Short answer:** UK public buyers are putting transport and logistics work out to tender all the time, and much of it suits fleet, courier, haulage, removals and freight SMEs. At the time of writing (2 November 2026), [DealAtlas](/categories/transport-logistics) had 23 live, open transport and logistics opportunities with a closing date still ahead: 14 for transport services, 7 for supporting transport and travel services, and 2 for transport equipment. Most of them close within weeks, so the firms that win are the ones who see them early.

This guide shows what's being bought, the value bands and regions involved, and how to filter it all on DealAtlas.

## Internal transport and handling equipment

Public buyers also buy the equipment that moves goods and people inside a site, such as handling and internal transport equipment for depots, warehouses, hospitals and estates. DealAtlas files this under transport equipment. At the time of writing (2 November 2026) there were 2 open transport equipment opportunities, both nationwide, one in the £50k–£100k band and one in the £5m–£10m band.

If you supply, lease or maintain this kind of kit:

1. Open the [transport and logistics category](/categories/transport-logistics) and look for transport equipment.
2. Check the value band against what you can deliver and service.
3. Treat nationwide opportunities as reachable even if you're regional, because delivery locations are often spread out.
4. Save the search on [DealAtlas Pro](/pricing) so new equipment opportunities come to you.
