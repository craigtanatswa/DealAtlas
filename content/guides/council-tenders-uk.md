## What counts as a council tender?

A council tender is a contract a local authority puts out to suppliers. Local authorities include county, district, borough, city and unitary councils, London boroughs and combined authorities. Council procurement follows the [Procurement Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/54/contents), which came into force on 24 February 2025. The government's [guidance on the Act](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents) explains the rules councils work to.

Councils must publish a notice for contracts of £30,000 or more. Above £207,720 for goods and services, or £5,193,000 for works, the full rules of the Act apply.

You'll need to register on the government's central digital platform before you bid, so find the right opportunity on [DealAtlas](/deals) first.
