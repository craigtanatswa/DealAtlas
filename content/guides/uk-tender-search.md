## Why discovery feels scattered after the Procurement Act 2023

The Act fixed where new opportunity notices must be published for most buyers in England, Wales and Northern Ireland: on the central digital platform ([Central digital platform factsheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-short-guides/central-digital-platform-factsheet-html)). That is a clear legal duty for buyers. It does not mean every useful signal for an SME sits in one search box you already use every Monday morning.

Three layers still sit beside new Act notices:

- Older-regime records. Procurements that started under the Public Contracts Regulations 2015 still carry award and call-off information under those saved rules. [PPN 019](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ppn-019-requirements-to-publish-on-contracts-finder/update-to-legal-requirements-to-publish-procurement-information-on-contracts-finder) sets out when those older publication duties still apply. From [1 May 2027](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/746/made), SI 2026/746 moves more of that older-regime duty onto the central digital platform.
- Devolved rules. Scottish authorities mostly buy under Scotland's own procurement law, so below-threshold Scottish notices follow different publication rules from the Act. Welsh and Northern Ireland buyers also have national arrangements alongside Act noticing where it applies.
- Buyer systems. A published notice is often only the front door. Documents, clarification questions and submissions usually run through the system the buyer names.

None of that is a reason to bounce between bookmarks as your main discovery habit. It is a reason to start from one hub that gathers what is live, then open the buyer's process when you decide to bid.

## Thresholds that decide what must be published in 2026

The contract's estimated value including VAT decides whether the full Act applies. [PPN 023](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ppn-023-2026-threshold-amounts/ppn-023-2026-threshold-amounts-html) sets the amounts for procurements that start on or after 1 January 2026 and run to 31 December 2027.

| Contract type | Threshold including VAT |
| --- | --- |
| Goods or services for a central government authority | £135,018 |
| Goods or services for any other public buyer | £207,720 |
| [Works](/categories/construction-infrastructure) | £5,193,000 |
| Light touch contracts | £663,540 |
| Utilities goods or services (not works) | £415,440 |

A contract at or above its threshold is a public contract and needs the full noticing sequence on the central digital platform. Below the threshold, lighter rules apply. If a buyer advertises a notifiable below-threshold contract, it must first publish a below-threshold tender notice on the central digital platform. That duty applies at £12,000 including VAT for most central government authorities, including NHS trusts and foundation trusts, and at £30,000 for other buyers ([Below-threshold contracts guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-define-phase/guidance-below-threshold-contracts-html)). A contract details notice is still required after award at those same values, even when the buyer never advertised the opportunity.

The higher works threshold is why many [construction and infrastructure](/categories/construction-infrastructure) contracts are advertised under the below-threshold rules.

## FAQ

### Why is UK public tender discovery scattered?

The Procurement Act 2023 requires buyers in England, Wales and Northern Ireland to publish required notices on the central digital platform, while older-regime records, devolved rules and buyer submission systems still sit alongside that duty. SMEs often need more than one official record to understand the market, which is why a searchable hub helps.

### What changed for new notices after 24 February 2025?

New procurements under the Procurement Act 2023 need their opportunity notices on the central digital platform. Award information for procurements that started under the previous rules can still appear under those older publication duties until the saved rules change again from 1 May 2027.

### Which 2026 thresholds decide whether a notice must be published?

For procurements that start between 1 January 2026 and 31 December 2027, the main thresholds including VAT are £135,018 for central government goods and services, £207,720 for other public buyers, £5,193,000 for works, £663,540 for light touch contracts and £415,440 for utilities goods or services. Advertised below-threshold contracts at or above £12,000 (most central government authorities) or £30,000 (other buyers) also need a notice on the central digital platform.
